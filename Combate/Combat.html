How Pokémon Battles Work:

  1. Starting a Battle:
    - A Pokémon battle begins when two trainers encounter each other or when a trainer encounters a wild Pokémon.

  2. Choosing Pokémon:
    - Each trainer picks which Pokémon they want to use in the battle from their team of up to six Pokémon.

  3. Turn-Based Actions:
    - The battle happens in turns. Each trainer takes turns giving commands to their Pokémon.

  4. Commands and Moves:
    - Trainers choose actions like attacks, healing, or other moves for their Pokémon to perform. Each Pokémon can use up to four different moves.

  5. Combat Mechanics:
    - Moves can have different effects like causing damage, making the opponent weaker, or changing the battle conditions.
    - Some moves are stronger against certain types of Pokémon (like fire being strong against grass), so choosing the right move is important.

  6. Status Conditions:
    - Pokémon can also be affected by conditions like being poisoned or asleep, which can change how they perform in battle.

  7. Ending the Battle:
    - The battle ends when one trainer defeats all of the other trainer's Pokémon by lowering their health to zero.

  8. Strategy and Winning:
    - Trainers need to think about the types of Pokémon they use and which moves to use to win the battle.
    - Winning battles earns trainers rewards like experience points for their Pokémon to get stronger.
Pokémon battles are all about strategy, choosing the right Pokémon and moves, and trying to outsmart the opponent to win. It's a fun and challenging part of playing Pokémon games!
